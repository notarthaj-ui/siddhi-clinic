About the clinic

Care built around mothers & children

For years, families in Takka Colony and across Panvel have trusted Siddhi Clinic and Nursing Home for dependable obstetric, gynaecological and paediatric care.

Our Mission

To provide safe, respectful and affordable maternity and child healthcare to every family in Panvel, with the same warmth we'd want for our own.

Our Vision

To be Panvel's most trusted name in obstetrics, gynaecology and paediatrics — known for clinical excellence and genuine patient care.

Why Choose Us

Experienced specialist doctors, fair trial for normal deliveries, an in-house nursing home, and a team that remembers your name.

What we treat

Our Services

From pregnancy to paediatrics, comprehensive care for every stage of your family's journey.

Obstetrics & Delivery

Full antenatal care with fair trial for normal delivery, plus safe cesarean section when needed.

Gynaecology

Comprehensive women's health checks, treatment and consultations from a DGO-qualified specialist.

Paediatrics

Growth monitoring, illness care and childhood vaccination from a doctor with 16+ years of experience.

Laparoscopy & Hysterectomy

Minimally invasive surgical procedures performed with modern equipment and post-op care.

Family Planning & MTP

Confidential family planning counselling at our government-recognised MTP centre.

In-house Nursing Ward

Comfortable general ward facilities for admitted patients, with round-the-clock nursing support.
